# Language file: Japanese (ja)

- **code:** ja
- **name:** Japanese
- **script:** Kanji + kana
- **reading:** kana
- **speech:** ja-JP
- **levels:** N5, N4, N3, N2, N1
- **level direction:** lower number = harder (N5 easiest, N1 hardest)
- **goal:** N3
- **test date:** 2027-02-28
- **start level:** N5

## Notes
- JLPT scale: **N5 easiest, N1 hardest** (the reverse of HSK). Always say which scale you mean.
- Library files: `ja-vocab-n5.tsv` ... `ja-vocab-n1.tsv` (columns: word, reading, meaning, section, example, translation) and `ja-vocab-starter-with-examples.tsv`. Examples are mostly empty; write them natively.
- Some N4/N3 entries use rare kanji spellings for words normally written in kana. Teach the usual form when relevant.
- Exam structure: `ja-exam-specs.md` (approximate; the official pass marks are N5 80, N4 90, N3 95, N2 90, N1 100 out of 180, with a minimum per section).
- Learner: reading strong (kanji from Chinese), listening and speaking weak, conjugation and exceptions (day counts, month names, counters) shaky.
