# Chinese exam structures (new HSK)

Official HSK sample papers (HSK 5, 6, 7-9) and the 2025-11 syllabus supplied by the learner. Counts and timings are from the sample paper cover pages.

## HSK 5
Total questions: 72
- **listening:** 35 questions, 25 min — Q1-19 short items; Q20-35 longer dialogues/passages
- **reading:** 35 questions, 35 min — Q36-45 cloze (words/phrases); Q46-55 paragraph ordering; Q56-70 passages
- **writing:** 2 tasks, 40 min — Q71 picture story >=100 chars; Q72 topic essay >=200 chars
- **speaking (separate oral paper):** 5 tasks, about 23 min incl. preparation — 2 listen-and-retell; 1 picture description; 2 answer questions

## HSK 6
Total questions: 82
- **listening:** 40 questions, 30 min — Q1-8; Q9-28; Q29-40
- **reading:** 40 questions, 40 min — Q41-50 word cloze; Q51-60 sentence cloze with one distractor; Q61-80 passages
- **writing:** 2 tasks, 45 min — Q81 application text >=150 chars; Q82 topic essay >=300 chars
- **speaking (separate oral paper):** 5 tasks, about 23 min incl. preparation

## HSK 7-9
Total questions: 91
- **listening:** 40 questions, 33 min
- **reading:** 40 questions, 50 min
- **writing:** 2 tasks, 55 min — Q81 chart description >=200 chars (15 min); Q82 topic essay >=600 chars (40 min)
- **translation:** 4 tasks, 42 min
- **speaking:** 5 tasks, 20 min

Scoring: HSK 5/6: listening, reading and writing are 100 points each (300 total). Check the official notice for the current pass mark.
